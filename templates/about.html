This application provides useful information about Chilimo Forest and its natural resources.

Visitors can learn about the history of the forest, wild animals, birds, plants, attractions, and location.

The system is designed to help visitors understand and explore the natural beauty and importance of Chilimo Forest.

System Features

  • Forest History
  • Wild Animals Information
  • Birds Information
  • Plants Information
  • Tourist Attractions
  • Forest Location
  • English and Afaan Oromoo Language Support
